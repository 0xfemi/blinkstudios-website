Eligibility

Who can apply?

Film and television professionals who already have experience in their field: producers, directors, editors, writers, cinematographers, sound editors, composers, VFX artists, post-production supervisors and other practitioners.

Do I need professional experience?

Yes. This is not primarily an entry-level training programme. It is designed for professionals who want to deepen their expertise and expand their opportunities.

Is the programme open internationally?

Yes. Mentoring happens across borders, with mentees and mentors in different countries.

Can I apply if I am not based in Nigeria?

Yes. You can apply from anywhere.

Mentors & matching

How are mentors selected?

Mentors are experienced professionals with relevant industry knowledge. The programme favours quality over quantity. Apply to mentor

How are mentors matched?

Mentees are matched with mentors based on their discipline, goals and the areas they want to develop.

After the programme

What happens after the programme?

Mentees join the alumni community and stay connected to mentors, peers and future opportunities.

Will participation guarantee employment or production credits?

No. The programme builds skills, networks and readiness for larger opportunities. It does not guarantee work or credits.
