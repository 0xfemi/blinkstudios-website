Privacy Policy

How the Blink Cross-Border Mentoring Programme collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this site, apply, mentor, partner with us or get in touch.

PoliciesPrivacy PolicyTerms of UseSafeguarding PolicyCode of ConductAccessibility Statement

Draft for review. This text sets out what the policy should cover and must be checked and approved by Blink Studios and its legal adviser before launch. Highlighted items need confirming.

Status: draftLast updated: October 2026Applies to: programme.blinkstudios.co

01Who we are

The Blink Cross-Border Mentoring Programme is run by Blink Studios with ScreenCraft Works. Blink Studios is responsible for personal information collected through programme.blinkstudios.co.

Registered company name and address: to be confirmed. Data protection contact: email to be confirmed.

02What we collect

We only collect what we need to run the programme.

WhenWhat we collect
You apply as a menteeName, email, phone, country, city, role, discipline, years of experience, organisation, LinkedIn, IMDb and website links, areas of interest, development goals, CV, showreel and work samples.
You apply to mentorName, email, country, role, experience, credits, areas of expertise, availability, mentoring experience, profile links and CV.
You make a partnership enquiryOrganisation, your name, role, email, country, type of support and your message.
You contact us or sign up for updatesName, email, topic and message, or your email address only.
You browse the sitePages visited, links and buttons clicked, approximate location by country, device and browser type.

03How we use it

  • To assess applications and match mentees with mentors.
  • To run the programme: orientation, sessions, workshops, events and the alumni community.
  • To send confirmation emails and reply to messages.
  • To send programme updates and event invitations, if you ask for them.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it, including counting applications, mentor applications, partnership enquiries and event registrations.
  • To report on the programme's impact to partners, using totals and anonymised figures unless you agree otherwise.

04Our legal basis

Depending on where you are, we rely on your consent, on our legitimate interest in running and improving the programme, and on steps needed before you take part. The laws that apply may include the UK GDPR and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Applicable laws to be confirmed by legal adviser.

05Who we share it with

  • ScreenCraft Works, which co-runs the programme.
  • Your matched mentor or mentee, who receives the parts of your application relevant to the match.
  • Service providers that host the site, store applications and send email. Provider names to be listed.
  • Nobody else, unless the law requires it. We never sell personal information.

06International transfers

The programme connects people across borders, so your information may be viewed in countries other than your own, including Nigeria, the United Kingdom and the United States. Where the law requires it, we put safeguards in place before transferring information.

07How long we keep it

Mentee and mentor records: period to be confirmed. Unsuccessful applications: period to be confirmed. Enquiries and messages: period to be confirmed. Update subscriptions: until you unsubscribe.

08Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, including for updates.
  • Complain to your data protection regulator, such as the ICO in the UK or the NDPC in Nigeria.

To use any of these rights, contact us.

09Cookies and analytics

The site uses analytics to count visits and actions. Analytics provider and cookie list to be confirmed. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site still works without them.

10Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell mentees and mentors by email.
