Why support

Invest in the people behind the screen

Sponsorship funds mentoring, workshops and the operations that keep cohorts running, and puts your organisation alongside the next generation of global film and television talent.

Why support

  • Direct access to a pipeline of experienced, vetted professionals
  • Visible commitment to under-represented production and post talent
  • Presence at Cross-Border Conversations, workshops and showcases
  • Reporting on mentees, matches and outcomes

Ways to structure your support

Programme partner

Supports a full cohort of mentees.

  • Named partner on the programme
  • Impact reporting
  • Industry session slot

Mentee sponsor

Supports a set number of mentee places in a cohort.

  • Named on supported places
  • Masterclass opportunity

Event partner

Supports a Cross-Border Conversation, workshop or social.

  • Co-branded event
  • Speaker slot

In-kind partner

Provides software, equipment, venues, training or production placements.

  • Listed as a supporter
  • Tailored recognition
