Programme journey seven stages

A designed experience from first form to final project

Each stage has a clear purpose, so mentees always know where they are and what comes next.

Dates, session counts and duration to be confirmed by the programme team.

  1. Stage 1

    Application

    You submit your experience, portfolio and development goals.

  2. Stage 2

    Selection

    The programme team shortlists applicants who fit the programme.

  3. Stage 3

    Mentor matching

    You're paired with a mentor by discipline and the areas you want to develop.

  4. Stage 4

    Orientation

    An introduction to the programme, your mentor and how sessions run.

  5. Stage 5

    Mentorship

    Regular, structured sessions focused on your goals.

  6. Stage 6

    Workshops & industry sessions

    Group sessions, masterclasses and Cross-Border Conversations with industry guests.

  7. Stage 7

    Project & practical development

    Where possible, learning is applied to real work and industry situations.

Who it's for

Producers, Directors, Editors, Writers, Cinematographers, Sound editors, Composers, VFX artists, Post-production supervisors, and other film & TV practitioners
