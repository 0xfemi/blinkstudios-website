The big idea

Talent exists everywhere. We're building the pathways to help it go further.

Film and television professionals across Africa, the Caribbean and beyond already have the talent and the experience. What's often missing is access: the networks, the practical guidance and the opportunities that lead to work at a higher level.

  • Strengthen existing skills
  • Develop new areas of expertise
  • Gain practical industry insight
  • Build international relationships
  • Understand global production standards
  • Prepare for larger, more complex projects

FAQs

Before you apply

All questions →
Who can apply?

Film and television professionals who already have experience in their field: producers, directors, editors, writers, cinematographers, sound and post professionals, distribution professionals and other practitioners.

Do I need professional experience?

Yes. This is not an entry-level training programme. It is designed for people who want to deepen existing expertise and step up to larger work.

Can I apply if I'm not based in Nigeria?

Yes. The programme is cross-border by design, with mentees and mentors in different countries.

Will it guarantee employment or production credits?

No. The programme builds skills, networks and readiness for larger opportunities. It does not guarantee work or credits.
