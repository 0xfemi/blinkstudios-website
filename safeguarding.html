Safeguarding Policy

Our commitment to keeping everyone in the programme safe, online and in person, and how to raise a concern.

PoliciesPrivacy PolicyTerms of UseSafeguarding PolicyCode of ConductAccessibility Statement

Draft for review. This text sets out what the policy should cover and must be checked and approved by Blink Studios and its legal adviser before launch. Highlighted items need confirming.

Status: draftLast updated: October 2026Applies to: programme.blinkstudios.co

01Our commitment

Everyone taking part in the programme has the right to feel safe and respected. We take every concern seriously and act on it promptly.

02Who this covers

Mentees, mentors, programme staff, volunteers, partners and guests, in every programme activity: one-to-one sessions, workshops, Cross-Border Conversations, socials and in-person meetings.

03Safe mentoring sessions

  • Sessions take place on agreed video platforms or in public or professional settings.
  • Either person can pause or end a session at any time.
  • Mentors don't ask for money, favours or personal information beyond what the mentoring needs.
  • Any business arrangement between a mentor and mentee, such as paid work, is disclosed to the programme team first.
  • Recording a session requires the agreement of everyone in it.

04Designated safeguarding lead

Name: to be confirmed. Contact: to be confirmed. Deputy: to be confirmed.

05How to raise a concern

  • Use the contact form and choose "Safeguarding concern". Only the safeguarding lead sees these messages.
  • Or email safeguarding address to be confirmed.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

06What happens next

  • We acknowledge your concern within timeframe to be confirmed.
  • We assess it, keep it confidential and share it only with those who need to know to keep people safe.
  • We may pause a mentorship while we look into it, and we tell you what we've done where we can.
  • Nobody who raises a concern in good faith will be penalised for it.
