Code of Conduct

What we expect from mentees, mentors, staff, partners and guests across the programme.

PoliciesPrivacy PolicyTerms of UseSafeguarding PolicyCode of ConductAccessibility Statement

Draft for review. This text sets out what the policy should cover and must be checked and approved by Blink Studios and its legal adviser before launch. Highlighted items need confirming.

Status: draftLast updated: October 2026Applies to: programme.blinkstudios.co

01Respect and inclusion

  • Treat everyone with respect across cultures, countries, disciplines and levels of experience.
  • No harassment, bullying, discrimination or unwelcome behaviour of any kind, online or in person.
  • Be aware of time zones, language and connectivity differences when you work across borders.

02Confidentiality

  • Projects, scripts, cuts and business information shared in mentoring are confidential.
  • Don't share another person's work or ideas without their permission.
  • Don't use a mentee's ideas or contacts for your own projects without agreement and credit.

03Professional boundaries

  • Keep the relationship professional and focused on the mentee's goals.
  • Disclose conflicts of interest, such as hiring or being hired by your match, to the programme team.
  • Follow the Safeguarding Policy for every session.

04Showing up

  • Attend agreed sessions and give notice if you need to reschedule.
  • Come prepared, with goals and work to discuss.
  • Give honest, constructive feedback, and take it in the same spirit.

05If the code is broken

Depending on what happened, we may talk it through, issue a warning, end a mentorship or remove someone from the programme and its events. Serious concerns follow the safeguarding process.

To report a breach, contact the team.
